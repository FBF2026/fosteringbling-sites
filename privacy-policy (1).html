1Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to all websites, platforms, and services operated by Fostering Bling Foundation and HavenAssist, including HavenAssist CW (caseworkers), HavenAssist FP (foster parents), HavenAssist FC (foster children), fosteringbling.org, havenassist.org, and birthdaybling.org.

2Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Name and email address (pilot signups, newsletters, contact forms)
  • Organization name and role (caseworker and agency accounts)
  • Donation information (processed securely through PayPal)
  • Case notes and documentation generated using HavenAssist tools

Automatically collected:

  • Browser type, device information, pages visited, IP address (anonymized)

⚠ HavenAssist FC collects only a first name and age group for personalization. No location data, no identifying information, and no data is shared without explicit consent.

3How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve our AI tools and services
  • Process donations and send tax receipts
  • Send program updates and service announcements (opt out any time)
  • Respond to support requests
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements

💙 We will never sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising. HavenAssist and Fostering Bling Foundation exist to serve child welfare — not to monetize your data.

4Children's Privacy

The protection of children's privacy is central to everything we do. For HavenAssist FC (ages 6–18):

  • No collection of location data from minors under any circumstances
  • No photographs or identifying images collected without guardian consent
  • Journal entries are private and encrypted — accessible only to the child
  • Caseworker messaging is logged for child safety and documentation only
  • No advertising directed at children
  • No sale or sharing of minor data with third parties

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable Illinois state laws governing minors in the child welfare system.

⚠ If you believe a child's information has been collected inappropriately, contact us immediately at Senett@fosteringbling.org

5Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted partners who help operate our platforms (hosting, payments, email) — bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect the safety of a child
  • Child safety escalations: Urgent alerts in HavenAssist FC are shared with the child's designated caseworker as part of the platform's safety design
  • Grant reporting: Aggregated, anonymized data only — never individual identifying information

6Data Security

  • SSL/HTTPS encryption on all websites and platforms
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive case documentation
  • Private journal encryption for HavenAssist FC users
  • Limited access — only authorized personnel can access user data

If you believe your data has been compromised, contact Senett@fosteringbling.org immediately.

7Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to opt out of communications at any time. Contact Senett@fosteringbling.org with subject line "Privacy Request." We respond within 30 days.

8Cookies

We use minimal cookies for session management and anonymous analytics only. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

9Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — anonymous site analytics
  • Anthropic / Claude API — AI language processing for HavenAssist tools
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Squarespace — domain management

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Significant changes will be communicated by email or site notice.
