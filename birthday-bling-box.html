The Most Important Item

The gem they keep forever.

"You are not forgotten. You are not invisible. You are a Diamond."

Every Birthday Bling Box includes a small gem keepsake — a physical reminder that they belong to something. That someone planned this for them. That they matter. Long after the balloons deflate, the gem remains.

How It Works

Simple. Beautiful. Delivered.

We handle everything. Foster families just have to celebrate.

1
Foster Parent Requests
Submit a simple request form with the child's birthday month, age, and any theme preferences.
2
Donor Sponsors the Box
A $50 donation from our community covers the full Birthday Bling Box — party supplies, cake card, and gem keepsake.
3
FBF Ships the Box
We pack and ship or deliver the Birthday Bling Box to arrive before the child's birthday month.
4
Diamond Celebrates 🎉
A foster child opens a box full of birthday magic — and knows someone planned this just for them.
