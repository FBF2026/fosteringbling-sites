✨ Why This Exists

Every Diamond Has a
Birthday Worth Celebrating

Foster children are our Diamonds — precious, resilient, and deserving of every sparkle life has to offer. But too often, birthdays pass without a box, a gift, or even a card.

The Fostering Bling Foundation started Birthday Bling Box to change that. But boxes cost money. Supplies cost money. Showing up consistently costs money.

"The Jewelry Box isn't just a donation. It's a promise. A promise that every Diamond in our care will know they are seen, celebrated, and loved — every single month of the year."
— Senett Basquine, Founder & CEO

When you join The Jewelry Box as a monthly sustainer, you become part of the setting that makes every Diamond shine. Your giving — small or large — funds birthday boxes, resources for foster families, and the tools caseworkers need to serve every child well.
