⚖️ My Rights

As a young person in foster care in Illinois, you have real rights. Here's what they are.

1
You have the right to be safe
No one is allowed to hurt you — physically, emotionally, or in any other way. If you don't feel safe, you can tell your caseworker, a teacher, or call the DCFS hotline.
2
You have the right to know your case plan
You have the right to understand what is happening in your case and what the plan is for your future.
3
You have the right to go to school
No placement change can interrupt your education. You have the right to stay in your school or get transportation to continue there.
4
You have the right to privacy
Your personal belongings, mail, and private conversations deserve respect. You also have the right to a private space in your home.
5
You have the right to contact your caseworker
You can contact your caseworker directly at any time. No one can prevent you from reaching your caseworker or DCFS.
6
You have the right to keep your belongings
If you move placements, your personal belongings move with you — in a proper bag, not a trash bag. Your things are yours.
7
You have the right to participate in court
You have a voice in court proceedings about your case. You can request to speak or have a CASA advocate speak for you.
8
You have the right to report concerns
If something feels wrong — in your placement, with your caseworker, or anywhere — you have the right to report it without fear of punishment.
